06 FAQ

Questions people ask before they call.

Does my electrical job need a permit?

Most electrical work in Ontario has to be filed with the Electrical Safety Authority (ESA) and inspected, from new circuits to panel changes. A licensed electrical contractor files the notification for you, and you'll know whether your job needs one before anything is booked.

Can my panel handle an EV charger?

Often, yes. It depends on your service size (commonly 100 A or 200 A) and what your home already draws. A load calculation answers it properly. If the panel is close to its limit, a load management device can sometimes avoid a full upgrade.

Why does my breaker keep tripping?

Usually one of three things: too much plugged into one circuit, a short in a cord, device or the wiring itself, or a ground fault. If it trips again with everything on that circuit unplugged, leave it off. That points to a wiring fault that needs an electrician.

Should I replace an old fuse box or knob-and-tube wiring?

Many home insurers ask about fuse panels, knob-and-tube and aluminum wiring when you buy or renew a policy, and some won't cover a home until they're dealt with. An inspection gives you a written answer on what's there and what it would take to replace.
